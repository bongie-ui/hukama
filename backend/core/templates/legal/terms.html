Terms of use

Last updated 7 November 2026

Hukama is a hackathon demonstration running on PayPal's sandbox. No real money moves, and it is not a licensed money transmission service. Don't use it to send real funds.

Using Hukama

  • A family's admin can invite other members. Funders add money with PayPal and set the rules for each mandate. Viewers can only see.
  • Money in a mandate can only be paid to payees Hukama has verified, and only when the mandate's signed rules allow it. The assistant can suggest and record requests, but it can never pay, approve or change rules.
  • Requests that look like fraud are held until a family member confirms with the person at home on their registered number.

Links without an account

  • Upload links let a supplier or school send quotes, invoices and photos after logging in with the PayPal account that receives the payment. Anything sent can only pay that business.
  • Shop links let someone at home choose groceries after entering a PIN the family gave them. Keep the PIN private; the family will never ask for it. Five wrong PINs turn the link off.
  • Pool links let anyone give to a family's funeral pool with PayPal.

Whoever holds a link should keep it private. Families can replace or turn off a link at any time.

Your responsibilities

  • Only upload documents and photos you are allowed to share, and don't send false quotes or invoices.
  • Don't try to get around the rules, the fraud checks or another family's data.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal and are subject to PayPal's own terms. Mobile money delivery, grocery packing and delivery, and billers are simulated in this demo and labelled as such.

No warranty

Hukama is provided as-is for demonstration. We may change, reset or stop the demo at any time.
